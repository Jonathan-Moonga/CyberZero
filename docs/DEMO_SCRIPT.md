# CyberZero primary demo

1. Open Dashboard. Confirm four summary cards and the `SIMULATION` safety banner.
2. Enable **Wake listening** in Agent Zero or Settings and approve microphone access. Run this once before presenting while online: the first use downloads the local voice model. Say **“Hey Agent Zero, simulate breach.”** Allow about two seconds for local transcription. Wake listening pauses while Zero speaks and automatically arms for the CEO's answer after each question.
3. Say **“Yes, Zero. Show me the attack.”** Open Detection and confirm the Floor 2 plan shows Operations Room 2B in red, with a red path from `203.0.113.42` to `CZ-OPS-204`.
4. Say **“Agent Zero, initiate Zero-Day Operation.”** Zero asks for CEO authorization.
5. Say the complete access-granted sentence with **“four, two, five, six, U as in University, T as in Texas, D as in Dallas.”** Partial or incorrect spoken codes do not execute the operation. The typed `4256UTD` fallback remains available.
6. Watch the local rocket sequence finish all five isolation steps. The transfer stops and the path changes state.
7. Say **“Let's set up the backup system.”** Create a backup and verify its SHA-256 manifest.
8. Invite Olivia Torres as Operations Managing Director. Photon is visibly queued and the recovery phrase appears only to the CEO.
9. Switch to the Operations Director profile, enter the phrase, and decrypt the Operations backup.
10. Open `inventory.json` and `vendor-status.csv` in the read-only preview. Note that Finance, HR, and Executive remain inaccessible.
11. In Settings, show Ollama health, voice selection, persistent wake listening, truthful service statuses, and permanently enabled Safe Demo Isolation Mode.

All employees, contact details, events, systems, and network addresses shown in the demo are fictional or documentation-reserved.
